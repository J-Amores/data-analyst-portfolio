1. Ambiguous Spend Drivers BLIND SPOT #1

Inability to discern whether spending surges stem from epidemiological demand (claim volume) or manufacturer price escalation (unit cost inflation). Without rigorous decomposition, programs risk curbing patient access instead of penalizing supplier price hikes.

2. Generic Substitution Leakage BLIND SPOT #2

Reimbursing premium brand medications despite bioequivalent multi-source generic alternatives active in the market. The lack of point-of-sale generic enforcement squanders tens of millions in avoidable brand-spread subsidization.

3. Lack of Enforceable Price Ceilings BLIND SPOT #3

Absence of empirical price dispersion benchmarks to set Maximum Allowable Cost (MAC) caps and negotiate supplemental rebates. Reimbursing at average wholesale benchmarks allows high-cost generic labelers excessive margin spread.

SPECIFICATION Medicaid Drug Spend Optimization — Technical Architecture & Hypothesis Validation

1. Data Engineering & CLEAN Architecture

Audits the CMS Medicaid Spending by Drug (RY2026 release, CY2020–CY2024 longitudinal panel). Raw source files interleave national composite summary rows (Mftr_Name == 'Overall') with individual labeler details. The production pipeline (src/etl/clean_pipeline.py) isolates these rows into partitioned Parquet stores (data/cleaned/mcd_drug_overall_2020_2024.parquet and data/cleaned/mcd_mftr_detail_2020_2024.parquet), preventing catastrophic 100% budget double-counting. Suppressed cells (<11 claims) are preserved as NaN/NULL.

2. Hypothesis 1: Pareto Spend Concentration (Lorenz Curve Analysis)

Evaluates gross expenditure across 4,774 unique drug formulations ($111.3B outlay). Cumulative spend distribution confirms severe Pareto concentration (Gini coefficient = 0.9028, Kolmogorov-Smirnov D = 0.7609, p < 10^-99, Reject H0). The top 5% of distinct drug entities account for 65.4% of total outpatient spend, with top 75 formulations driving over 50% of all Medicaid outpatient drug spending.

3. Hypothesis 2: Logarithmic Price vs. Volume Decomposition

For all 3,762 drugs with complete longitudinal panels from 2020 to 2024, spend expansion is decomposed via:
Δln(Spend) = Δln(Price) + Δln(Volume)
Across top accelerating categories with >50% expenditure expansion, Unit Cost CAGR accounts for 72.4% of budget expansion, while claim volume expansion accounts for only 27.6% (paired t-test t = -7.97, p = 2.79e-12; Wilcoxon signed-rank W = 252.0, p = 5.48e-15).

4. Hypothesis 3: Multi-Source Generic Price Dispersion & MAC Modeling

Across 1,525 multi-source formulations and 759 multi-source generic cohorts (10,435 labeler detail records where Tot_Mftr >= 2), competing manufacturers exhibit an interquartile price dispersion exceeding 35% (CV = 78.28%, Wilcoxon W = 211,957, p = 1.78e-29). Establishing mandatory State Maximum Allowable Cost (MAC) price ceilings pegged to 25th-percentile benchmark pricing extracts $100M+ in recurring annual savings while maintaining full supply redundancy (<2.5% chronic therapy disruption).
