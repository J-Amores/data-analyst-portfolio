Key Evidence & Context COST DRIVER REVIEW
1. Unit Prices vs. Patient Volume 72.4% PRICE DRIVEN

Clinical teams assumed budget increases came from higher patient volume. The data shows unit prices drove nearly three quarters of the increase.

2. Diabetes Dose Cost Surge 5X SPEND MULTIPLIER

For diabetes treatments, prescription counts rose 18%, but cost per dose rose 84%. Price increases drove five times more spending than patient volume did.

3. Specialty Infusions Price Creep STEADY VOLUME

Patient volume held steady for several high-cost infusions, yet annual hospital spend climbed because manufacturers raised prices each year.

4. Predictable Volume, Controllable Price ACTIONABLE LEVER

Our volume is predictable. The real issue is the wholesale price we pay per dose.

Core Purchasing Inefficiencies 4 AUDIT FINDINGS
1. Unconverted Brand-Name Orders $1.1M OVERPAYMENT

The hospital still orders and dispenses brand-name medications when FDA-approved bioequivalent generics are available.

2. Wide Multi-Source Generic Price Spread >35% VARIANCE

For generic drugs with multiple manufacturers, wholesale prices vary by more than 35% for the identical active ingredient.

3. Ad-Hoc Bottle Selection $2.1M OVERPAYMENT

Without strict rules in purchasing, buyers often order whichever generic bottle is in stock, paying a markup for the packaging.

4. Supplier Standardization Opportunity SAME EFFICACY

Standardizing suppliers across these bioequivalent drugs lowers costs without changing clinical efficacy.

Contract Tier Compliance and Automatic Substitution Recoup $3.2M Annually

Projected Annual Recovery Across Two Hospital Policy Adjustments

Projected Savings Waterfall Chart +$2.1M GPO TIERING + $1.1M AUTO SUBSTITUTION = $3.2M NET RECOVERY
Key Policy Levers STANDARDIZED POLICY
1. GPO Contract Tier Enforcement ($2.1M) +$2.1M / YR

Requiring buyers to order generics at our 25th-percentile contracted price stops wholesaler overpayment on routine stock.

2. Automatic Generic Substitution ($1.1M) +$1.1M / YR

Setting our order entry system to substitute bioequivalent generics automatically stops brand overpayment at discharge.

3. Supply Chain Reliability Safeguard 25TH-PCT CAP

Capping generic purchase prices at the 25th percentile of contracted suppliers keeps order volumes reliable and prevents supply shortages.

4. Clinical Safety Threshold Verified 1.8% DISRUPTION

Modeled changes affect 1.8% of chronic care regimens, staying safely below our 2.5% clinical safety threshold.
