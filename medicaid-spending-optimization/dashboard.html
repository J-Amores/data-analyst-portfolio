Guardrail 1: Therapy Continuity 1.8% < 2.5%

Limits formulary switching strictly to bioequivalent oral/inhaler formulations with active clinician override options in the EHR.

Guardrail 2: Outlier Cleaning 0.0% Distortion

Excludes CMS packaging outliers (affecting 1,738 formulations and 5.07% of the raw wedge) to eliminate artificial margin overstatements.

Guardrail 3: Dosage Matching Rule ISS-05

Isolates depot keywords (e.g. Maintena, Sustenna, Lupron, Vivitrol) so long-acting injectables are never matched against oral generic tablets.
