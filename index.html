Why Call Duration Is a Trap in Pre-Call Lead Scoring

Tutorial models boast 90%+ accuracy on bank marketing data by feeding call length (duration) into the classifier. Because a sales manager cannot know how long a call will last before dialing, we stripped duration from pre-call scoring and used it strictly post-call to uncover 475 hours of wasted repeat dialing.

REFLECTION 02 • FINTECH RISK 33 PYTESTS

The Active-Loan Blind Spot Hiding a 20% Default Rate

Standard LendingClub summaries mix 912,569 unfinished active loans into the 2.26M raw database, making the portfolio default rate look like a safe 12%. Isolating the 1.35M completed loans revealed the true 20% charge-off rate—1.7x higher than reported—and pinpointed the $230M subprime leak.

Read Credit Risk Case Study
REFLECTION 03 • HEALTHCARE OPS 55 PYTESTS

Stopping a $223B Double-Count in CMS Drug Ledgers

CMS Part D & Medicaid tables interleave national Overall rollup rows directly alongside individual manufacturer rows. Summing the raw table inflates national drug spend to $223B instead of the true $111B, and replacing HIPAA <11 blanks with zeros fabricates artificial volume spikes.

Read Pharmacy Spend Case Study
REFLECTION 04 • E-COMMERCE 32 PYTESTS

Why Dropping Null Customer IDs Deletes £1.5M in Guest Carts

Most retail basket tutorials run df.dropna(subset=['CustomerID']) in step one—unknowingly wiping out 135,080 guest checkout rows (25% of all orders and £1.5M in revenue). Grouping by Invoice ID preserved guest carts while isolating 9,288 credit-note cancellations to screen bundle return risk.
