Item 01: The High-Interest Trap
"Charging 20% to 26% interest on Grades E through G looks profitable on paper. In practice, 40% to 50% of those borrowers default early, so we lose the original loan principal before collecting enough interest."
Item 02: The 5-Year Payback Penalty
"Five-year (60-month) loans make up 24.1% of lending ($6.58B) but default at 32.45% (double the 16.00% rate of 3-year loans), cutting net returns in half (+2.57% vs. +5.12%)."
Item 03: Unsecured Small Business Losses
"While credit card refinancing earns a strong +5.51% return, small business loans hit a 29.71% default rate and lose money (-0.59% return) because applicants do not have to submit tax returns to verify business revenue."

"When a borrower already spends over 25% of their monthly income on debt payments and has maxed out over 65% of their credit card limit, they have no financial cushion if an unexpected expense comes up."

Option A: Blanket Ban on All Subprime Loans (Grades D-G)
Eliminates 28.45% of lending volume ($5.52B)
FAILS our >= 80.0% volume guardrail and turns away $4.1B in loans that actually get paid back.
Option B: Targeted Rule (Decline Grades D-G only when DTI > 25% & Card Usage > 65%)
Prunes just 3.55% of loans (47,705 accounts defaulting at 38.88%)
PASSES our volume guardrail at 95.81% kept while saving +$230.0M in net unpaid losses.
