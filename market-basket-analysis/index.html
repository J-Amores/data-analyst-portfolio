Decision-Driven Architecture

4-Layer Metric Hierarchy & Guardrails

A balanced measurement system preventing vanity metric optimization by enforcing reverse logistics cost controls and cannibalization accounting.

North Star Metric PRIMARY

Net AOV & Multi-SKU Basket Share: Expanding single-item retail checkouts into multi-line baskets (6+ items) to monetize strong wholesale and collection loyalty.

Association KPIs DISCOVERY

Support, Confidence & Lift: Statistical co-occurrence filtering (support >= 1.0%, chi-square p < 0.0001) isolating organic cross-purchase affinity from random transactional chance.

Guardrail 1: Quality RISK CEILING

SKU Return Rate < 5.0%: Hard operational ceiling. Automatically blocks high-return products from recommendation carousels to safeguard reverse shipping and warehouse intake costs.

Guardrail 2: Finance MARGIN FLOOR

Net Margin Retention: Requires net incremental profit > 0 after deducting cannibalized promotional discounts from organic buyers who would have co-purchased at full price.
